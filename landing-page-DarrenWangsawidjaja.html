Check the company before you apply. Cek dulu kantornya sebelum melamar.

Read reviews from people who actually worked there, see how they rate workload, pay, and managers, and catch red flags in a job post before you send your CV.

Baca ulasan dari orang yang benar-benar pernah bekerja di sana, lihat penilaian mereka soal beban kerja, gaji, dan atasan, lalu temukan red flag di lowongan sebelum kamu mengirim CV.

See what you can checkLihat yang bisa kamu cek

What you can checkYang bisa kamu cek

Verified, anonymous reviewsUlasan anonim terverifikasi

Only people who actually worked at a company can review it, and their names are never shown. Reviews appear once a company has at least three, so no one can tell who wrote what.

Hanya orang yang benar-benar pernah bekerja di sebuah perusahaan yang bisa mengulasnya, dan namanya tidak pernah ditampilkan. Ulasan baru muncul setelah ada minimal tiga, jadi tidak ada yang bisa menebak siapa menulis apa.

Work environment radarRadar lingkungan kerja

CekDulu reads every review aspect by aspect: workload, pay, managers, culture, career, and work-life balance. Each aspect gets a score, a short reason, and how confident that score is.

CekDulu membaca setiap ulasan per aspek: beban kerja, gaji, atasan, budaya, karier, dan work-life balance. Setiap aspek mendapat skor, alasan singkat, dan seberapa yakin skor itu.

Job post checkCek lowongan

Paste a job post and CekDulu marks the warning signs: a diploma held until the contract ends, fees before you start, pay below the regional minimum wage, or overtime with no extra pay.

Tempel teks lowongan, lalu CekDulu menandai tanda bahayanya: ijazah ditahan sampai kontrak selesai, biaya sebelum mulai kerja, gaji di bawah UMK, atau lembur tanpa tambahan upah.

How it worksCara kerja

  1. Workers reviewPekerja menilai

    People who worked there rate six aspects and describe what it was like.

    Orang yang pernah bekerja di sana menilai enam aspek dan menceritakan pengalamannya.

  2. CekDulu scoresCekDulu menghitung

    Star ratings are combined with what the review actually says. If the stars and the words disagree, a moderator takes a look.

    Bintang digabung dengan isi ulasan. Kalau bintang dan isinya bertentangan, moderator akan memeriksanya.

  3. You decideKamu memutuskan

    Compare companies and check job posts before you apply.

    Bandingkan perusahaan dan cek lowongan sebelum melamar.

  4. Companies respondPerusahaan menanggapi

    Companies can reply to reviews and show what they have fixed. Fixes confirmed by employees earn an “Improving” badge.

    Perusahaan bisa membalas ulasan dan menunjukkan apa yang sudah diperbaiki. Perbaikan yang dikonfirmasi karyawan mendapat badge “Membaik”.

About CekDuluTentang CekDulu

CekDulu, Indonesian for “check first”, helps job seekers avoid toxic workplaces by letting workers rate the places they work. It is built around SDG 8, target 8.8: safe and secure working environments for all workers. Fresh graduates and interns come first, because they have the least information and the most to lose from a bad first job.

CekDulu membantu pencari kerja menghindari kantor yang toxic dengan mengajak pekerja menilai tempat kerjanya. CekDulu dibangun untuk SDG 8, target 8.8: lingkungan kerja yang aman dan terlindungi bagi semua pekerja. Fresh graduate dan anak magang menjadi fokus pertama, karena merekalah yang paling sedikit punya informasi dan paling dirugikan oleh pekerjaan pertama yang buruk.
